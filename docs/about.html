About vanilla.penplotter

I wanted to try drawing on my iDraw with JavaScript, and to see the pen’s route before starting the machine.

Why

Keeping the drawing and the plotter connection in one browser page seemed useful. The sketch supplies points and pen assignments; the engine makes a plan you can inspect before plotting.

If you draw in p5.js, p5.penplotter connects your sketch to this engine.

How this was made

I use AI to help me build bridges to systems I would like to work with. I try to leave room for the joy of coding along the way.

This library was written with AI assistance, under my direction. I check changes in code and on my plotter, and I am responsible for what is here.

Sebastien Vanblaere · prjcts.be
