SVG to pen

You already have drawings as SVG: from p5.plotSvg, from Inkscape, from a text editor. Paste one here or pick the file. It gets read, fitted to the width you want, cleaned up and planned, and then it goes to the pen like everything else on this site. The usual detour through vpype and Inkscape is the part I wanted to skip.
