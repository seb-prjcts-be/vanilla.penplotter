Three lines, one at a time

Calculate a line, plot it and wait before calculating the next. The three lines are 5 mm long. One session returns to the starting corner after the last line.

sketch.js
Loading sketch.js...

For a physical plot, enter your paper corner's position from the machine origin. The lines start 12 mm inside that corner. Check that this area is clear.

Use an A4 sheet, with its corner at the entered position.

Ready for the screen demo.

After a stop or error, park the carriage at the machine origin before another plot.

The driver session was physically tested with three successive lines on the iDraw HSE / A2. This page uses the same API; the screen demo uses a log connection.
