The smallest complete job

First job

A hatched shape, its outline and a circle. Not much, but enough to watch the engine do its work: it joins the hatch lines end to end, orders them so the pen travels as little as possible, and shows you in red where it still has to lift. Then it goes to the pen.

Core snippet
plot.hatch(polygon, { spacing: 4.5, angle: Math.PI / 7 });
plot.polygon(polygon);
plot.circle(108, 145, 38);
const plan = plot.plan({ drawSpeed: 35, travelSpeed: 80 });
