vanilla.waves · a still from an animation

Waves to pen

vanilla.waves animates; a plotter draws once. So I stop time: every line is sampled at t = 0, and the 32 rows that would ripple on a screen become one still sheet. That is the whole trick for plotting anything that moves. Pick the moment, then go to the pen.
